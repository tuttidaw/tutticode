- **References (what is borrowed):**
  - Slum Village "Fall in Love", only the drum time-feel: straighter hats against swung, late kick and snare.
  - Miles Davis / Bill Evans "Blue in Green", only rootless voicings whose inner voices move by half-steps, and the licence for an odd-length (6-bar) middle.
- **Arc:** lamp on (I) → settling in (A1) → a line catches you (C1) → eyes leave the page (D, the turn) → back to the line, it means more (C2) → book closes, lamp still on (O).
- **Style split:** hip-hop owns groove, low end, form and mix; jazz owns harmony, melody and colour.

- The chorus answer is now one fixed cell: rest, head A5, quarter, dotted-quarter landing on the chord tone. In C2 the same cell starts from C6.
- The bell doubles every answer an octave below. The same answer opens the song in bar 2.
- Because the C1 climax answer was replaced, the song's high point moved to C2.

**2. The energy curve doesn't move.** **Accepted.**
- A soft kick and rim enter at bar 3, and bar 4 is a "stop" instead of a hole.
- Hats enter halfway through the verse (bar 9).
- At C1 the bell layer enters and the vinyl continues.
- In C2 the pad stays low and the bell is louder.
- The Outro steps down one layer per barline.
