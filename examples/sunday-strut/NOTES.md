# Sunday Strut

A funk/disco instrumental in A minor (dorian). 112 BPM, 4/4, 56 bars = 2:00, plus a 3 s ring-out.
Program: `song.tc`. Render: `render-final.wav`, made with `render.json` (−14 LUFS, limiter, tail 3 s).
Spec: `spec.yaml`; lines marked `[E]` were changed in stage E.

I cannot listen. Everything below is a reading of the notes, the skills' checklists and the render reports.

---

## Intent

One thing: **the bass struts in alone and, bar by bar, talks the whole band into following it.**

- **The bass riff is the arrangement hook.** It is the first sound at 0:00. It plays in every section and gets its own breakdown.
- **The lead is the voice.** It plays a call-and-response tune. Its chorus hook is a shout-back cell, R = E5 · E5-D5-C5, that a listener can echo after one hearing.
- **References** (borrowed role only, no notes):
  - Chic, *Good Times*: the bass as the melodic centre, plus a bass-and-drums stretch.
  - EWF, *September*: a short wordless hook with a held top note.

- One bar = 16 sixteenths. `.` is a 16th rest.
- `(n)` is a note length in sixteenths; no number means one 16th.
- Chord symbols sit above each bar.
- Lead sound: Voice VOX LEAD. Range E4–B5.

**Outro (bars 53–56).**

- **Bar 55:** the band plays the riff in unison on E.
- **Bar 56:** one Am9 hit on the One, then silence.

**Bass riff (the arrangement hook)**, over Am9:
- Notes: `A1(2) . A1 . . A2 G2 E2(2) . . . C2 D2 E2`.
- In plain terms: a long One, a push on the "a" of 1, an octave on the & of 2, then a climb into the next One.
- It is transposed to every chord root. Each climb lands a step or a fifth onto the next chord.

## Sounds and why

All sounds are TuttiDAW Ensemble/808 voices. Settings follow REFERENCE/lessons; no lesson notes are used.

| part | sound | why |
|---|---|---|
| bass | Ensemble **Duo SQR BASS**, lesson funk/main.tc settings (dec 76, sus 38, voice 1), level 110 | A short, round, finger-style tone that can carry a melodic riff. Dry; it is the star. Light sidechain (1.5:1) from the kick |
| kick, snare, hats, toms, crash | default 808 kit; ghost snares at velocity 0.2–0.26 | Tight and dry. The kit is the timing grid the bass pushes against |
| gtr | Ensemble **Physical PLUCK** (funk/horn-band settings) | A clean, muted rhythm-guitar scratch. Panned left, 8 ms behind the kit |
| clav | Ensemble **Digital FUNK KEY** (superstition lesson) + Chorus | A clavinet chick on the "&". Panned right (funk card: guitar and clav on opposite sides). Uses its own voicing table above the guitar |
| horns | Ensemble **Duo BRASS** (philly lesson) | Answers, stabs and unison shouts. Slap delay plus room |
| strings L/R | Ensemble **Duo STRINGS** (disco lesson), two voices with different attack/chorus settings, panned ±0.6, + Chorus | Disco strings above the band; the two lines are a third apart for width |
| lead | Ensemble **Voice VOX LEAD**, level 110 | The lessons use VOX LEAD for sung hooks; a bare saw read as "ringtone" (review-song issue 1) |
| leadhi | Ensemble **Duo SAW LEAD** (jazz-funk lesson) | A quiet octave-up double, C3 only: the register step for the last chorus |

Mix setup:
- Two buses: `room` (Reverb 1.3 s) and `slap` (Delay 402/268 ms, filtered).
- Master: Bus Compressor → Limiter.
- Push/pull is relative. The bass is on the grid; every other part is 12 ms late (gtr +20 ms, clav +16 ms). Reason: `.early()` wraps each section's first downbeat.
- Swing: `.swingBy(0.04, 8)` on every part.
